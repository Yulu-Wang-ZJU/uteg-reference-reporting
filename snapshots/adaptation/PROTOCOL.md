# Four-way adaptation comparison

Authorized scope: 15-minute HZ_to_SA and HZ+HB_to_SA, seeds 1000--1019. Fixed historical Ridge-10 source models, original 60-record target adaptation pools and identical 201-record test cohort. No source fitting, reporting gates, label-budget changes, threshold selection or test-driven tuning. Existing manuscript and figures remain unchanged.

Arms: raw source predictions; independent per-output affine least-squares calibration (unpenalized slopes/intercepts); joint linear calibration with squared loss and identity-centred penalty 10; historical selected complete adaptation (reuse, not reselect). All new calibration uses the original source-output standardization, training-pool centering and physical-range projection. Joint linear objective is sum of squared standardized residuals plus 10 times squared Frobenius distance of its matrix from identity. The affine fit has four independent slopes and intercepts. No hyperparameter search or best-arm selection is performed.

Fit cap: 80 adaptation procedures (two arms x two directions x 20 seeds); equivalently 200 regression solves when each of four affine outputs is counted separately. All new models must be saved and hashed before external prediction evaluation. Only adaptation-pool labels enter fitting. Original source/pool/test record and group separation is checked. Repeated seeds are not independent patients.

Endpoints before report selection: four-parameter MAE; MA three-state agreement; low/high to within-range counts divided by all true low/high record-seed events; normal to low/high; low/high correct classification and mutual confusion. Reference MA cutoffs remain 50 and 70 mm. This is not a gate/coverage experiment: all QC-eligible predictions are evaluated. Bootstrap 5,000 group-cluster resamples retain all 20 seeds per group and are paired across arms. Groups are recorded identity proxies, not independently verified patient identities.

Existing no-adaptation and complete-adaptation predictions are reused only after numerical reproduction of raw source predictions and identity checks. Existing Huber-only stage is used solely for implementation validation, not added as a fifth headline arm. Findings are exploratory external comparisons on an already examined cohort, not a new untouched confirmatory study. All arms and results are retained, irrespective of ranking.
