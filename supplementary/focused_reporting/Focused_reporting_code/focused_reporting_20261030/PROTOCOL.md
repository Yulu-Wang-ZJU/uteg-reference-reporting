# One bounded matched reporting comparison

## Admission and decision
The unresolved review question is whether the complete source-transfer workflow adds useful reporting capability beyond a simple target-only predictor. Existing median/Ridge comparisons concern ungated errors; existing risk comparisons hold the transferred predictions fixed. Neither supplies a matched target-only reporting interface. This comparison can distinguish reporting benefit of source transfer from comparable performance under local learning; it cannot establish sensor necessity, clinical utility or independent generalization.

Positive incremental results would support a limited reporting contribution. Similar results would remove a source-transfer superiority claim, without establishing equivalence or negating the common sensor/reporting approach. Unstable or unfavourable results leave the rejection rationale unresolved. No subsequent model/threshold search follows any outcome.

## Frozen scope
15-minute four-coefficient input; same60 SAHZU target-pool records,201 target test records,20 seeds1000–1019; same original outer/inner partitions. Target-only predictor is one shared comparator, not two independent source configurations. Compare it with saved HZ_to_SA and HZ+HB_to_SA two-sided-report actions. Original mappings, calibrators, risk heads, thresholds and actions remain unchanged.

For each original position A/B, evaluate Ridge penalties[0.1,1,10,100] using that position's original inner folds and MA absolute error; ties favour the larger penalty. Select only from A, fit on A and predict B. Cross-fitted predictions of the selected candidate on A train the original13-feature error-LR and B/MA low/high heads. This preserves the original architecture: candidate selection and risk training share A; only outer-B predictions and risks enter pooled development working-point selection. A cross-fitted value is not advertised as an unbiased validation score after candidate selection. Outer-B labels never select their position's predictor, preprocessing or risk model. Final position3 uses all60 target records and its original inner folds, matching the previous target-only final predictor. Verify final predictions/model coefficients against that frozen comparator.

All error-model and low/high-head mathematical functions, support checks, preprocessing, regularization and two-stage working-point selection are reused without changing settings. The13 feature semantics are preserved. Because target-only has no source model, output centring/scaling uses A's reference mean/SD; the subsequent fold-local risk-feature standardization removes this affine difference for nondegenerate finite columns. No source labels are consumed by the target-only learner. Verify feature-standardization invariance on synthetic finite data; report this normalization substitution explicitly.

Development normal rule: zero observed low/high-to-within, both abnormal classes represented and at least five distinct correct within releases; maximize within coverage. Then freeze within releases and maximize total coverage at agreement>75% and at least five distinct releases, using a common abnormal-score threshold. Original ties and unsupported-head fail-closed behaviour remain unchanged. No new clinical acceptability threshold.

Maximum1040 Ridge solves(20 seeds*4 positions*(4 penalties*3 folds+1 final fit)); at most80 error-LR and160 two-head fits. No source or calibration fits; no retries/search expansion. Freeze all models, developmental thresholds, code/protocol/input hashes before a separate test evaluation command. Preserve every seed, fallback, unsupported head and zero-report run. Private fitted objects stay local.

## Evaluation and interpretation
Primary quantities: correct/incorrect/unreported yields on all4020 record–seed events, coverage, agreement, low/high-to-within counts with full reference-class denominators and correct abnormal releases. Full3-by-4 destinations and per-seed values retained. Compare target-only minus each original workflow using5000 paired identity-proxy-group resamples retaining seeds; conditional descriptive intervals, not independent validation. Keep both marginal yield differences and conditional risk denominators; greater coverage alone is not improvement if errors increase. No unplanned composite utility or noninferiority margin, no test-selected operating point.

Report per-seed paired direction of correct/error/coverage changes and zero-report counts; seeds are repeated fits, not independent patients. Existing20-seed stability and three-time-schedule analyses are reused, not rerun. No additional timepoint. No manuscript update until results and checks are complete. No data upload, raw-data publication, original-output overwrite or claim that these already examined cohorts are independent.
